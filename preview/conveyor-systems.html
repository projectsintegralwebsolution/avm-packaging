What are Conveyor Systems?

Conveyor systems are material handling equipment used to transport products, cartons, bottles, bags, components and other materials between different stages of production. They help reduce repeated manual handling and support a smoother, more consistent production flow.

Industrial Conveyor Systems can be configured with different belt materials, conveyor dimensions, drive arrangements, speeds and automation controls depending on the product and manufacturing process.

Types of Conveyor Systems

Belt Conveyor Systems

Conveyor Belt Systems are used for continuous movement of products and packaged goods along a production or material-handling line. Belt material and conveyor dimensions can be selected according to the application.

Inclined Conveyor Systems

Inclined conveyors are used when products need to move between different working heights or production levels. The required incline and belt configuration depend on the product and transfer height.

Packaging Conveyor Systems

Packaging Conveyor Systems can be integrated with filling, sealing, labeling, coding and secondary packaging equipment to move products between processing stages.

Material Handling Conveyor Systems

A Material Handling Conveyor helps manufacturers move products and materials through production areas with less repeated manual handling and more consistent workflow.

How to Choose the Right Conveyor System

Choosing the right conveyor begins with understanding what needs to be moved and how the conveyor will fit into the production process. Conveyor dimensions, belt type and drive requirements should be selected around the actual application rather than using the same configuration for every factory.

  • Product Type & Dimensions: Identify the size, shape and surface of the product or package.
  • Load Requirement: Confirm the approximate weight per product and total load expected on the conveyor.
  • Required Output: Match conveyor speed with the production or packaging-line capacity.
  • Conveyor Length & Width: Select dimensions according to production layout and product size.
  • Belt Material: Choose a belt suitable for the product, operating environment and handling requirement.
  • Elevation: Confirm whether material needs to move horizontally or between different heights.
  • Operating Environment: Consider dust, moisture, temperature and other process conditions where relevant.
  • Automation: Decide whether the conveyor will operate independently or communicate with other machines in the line.

AVM Packaging System can review these application details and recommend a suitable Conveyor System configuration for the required production process.

Conveyor Automation Systems for Packaging Lines

Conveyor Automation Systems can connect multiple packaging operations so products move between machines with less manual transfer. Depending on the production process, conveyors can be integrated with Liquid Filling Machines, Sealing Machines and Carton Taping Machines.

The conveyor speed, height and product-guiding arrangement should be matched with the connected equipment so the overall line operates with a consistent product flow.
