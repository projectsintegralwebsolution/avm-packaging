How Does a Pad Printing Machine Work?

A pad printing machine transfers ink from an etched plate onto a product surface using a silicone pad. The flexible pad allows printing on curved, uneven, or textured surfaces with high precision and consistency.

Pad Printing Machine vs Screen Printing Machine

Pad printing machines are ideal for curved and uneven surfaces, while screen printing is better for flat surfaces. Pad printing offers higher precision and flexibility for industrial applications.
