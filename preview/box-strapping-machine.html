How Does a Box Strapping Machine Work?

The machine works by automatically feeding the strap around the box, tightening it to the required tension, and sealing it using heat or friction welding. This process ensures strong and consistent strapping for all types of cartons.

The machine is widely used in warehouses, manufacturing units, logistics centers and packaging industries where reliable box sealing is essential for safe transport.
