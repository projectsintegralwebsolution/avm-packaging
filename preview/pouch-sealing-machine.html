Key technical and purchasing information regarding our machinery.

A pouch sealing machine is used to securely seal plastic, aluminum foil, laminated, and paper pouches using precise thermal heat control. It ensures airtight, leak-proof, and tamper-evident packaging for food, pharmaceuticals, and industrial products.
An automatic pouch sealing machine feeds pouches via a conveyor belt through motorized heating blocks and cooling wheels. The heat melts the inner sealant layer, while compression rollers bond the pouch shut seamlessly in a fast, continuous motion.
The price of a pouch sealing machine in India starts from ₹15,000 for basic heavy-duty continuous sealers and ranges up to ₹2,500,000 or more for fully automatic, high-speed industrial pouch sealing machines with nitrogen flushing and batch coding features.
Pouch sealing machines can seal virtually all flexible materials, including Polyethylene (PE), Polypropylene (PP), Aluminum Foil, Laminated barrier pouches, Stand-up Pouches, Kraft Paper pouches, and BOPP films.
Continuous automatic pouch sealing machines can process 20 to 50 pouches per minute depending on the pouch length, thickness, material, and belt conveyor speed.
Automatic pouch sealing machines feature motorized conveyors, digital temperature controllers, and continuous sealing for mass production. Manual or foot-operated sealers seal one pouch at a time and require manual positioning by an operator.
