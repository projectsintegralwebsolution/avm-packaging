How Does a Shrink Tunnel Machine Work?

A shrink tunnel machine works by passing shrink-film-wrapped products through a heated chamber (the tunnel) mounted over a moving conveyor belt. Inside the tunnel, a set of heating elements and fans circulate hot air at a controlled temperature. The heat activates the shrink film — either PVC or POF — causing it to contract tightly and uniformly around the product's shape. The result is a taut, transparent, tamper-evident wrap that conforms precisely to the product contour.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Product Loading: The pre-wrapped product (wrapped in shrink film) is placed on the conveyor belt at the machine's infeed end.
  2. Conveyor Transport: The belt carries the product into the shrink tunnel at an adjustable, controlled speed.
  3. Heat Application: Inside the tunnel, hot air circulates evenly around the product, activating the film shrinkage.
  4. Film Contraction: The shrink film tightens uniformly around the product, eliminating wrinkles and loose film.
  5. Cooling & Exit: The product exits the tunnel and the film sets, creating a firm, professional shrink-wrapped package.

  • Inspect heating elements monthly for consistent temperature output
  • Lubricate conveyor belt rollers and drive chain as per the machine manual
  • Check temperature controller calibration periodically
  • Ensure blower fans are free from dust and debris for proper airflow
