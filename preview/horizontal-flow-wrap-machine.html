How Does a Horizontal Flow Wrap Machine Work?

The product is placed on the infeed conveyor, then wrapped in film as it passes through the forming box. The center sealing mechanism creates a longitudinal seal, while end seals cut and seal each pouch. This process is similar to how our vertical form fill sealing machine works, but optimized for solid, flat products. For dairy and cup products, see our manual cup sealing machine.
